Artisanal Craftsmanship

Our Almond Croissants are a labor of love, requiring 48 hours of preparation to achieve the perfect texture and flavor profile. Each pastry starts with premium French cultured butter, laminated carefully by hand into our sourdough-enhanced dough.

The filling is a traditional Frangipane, crafted from stone-ground California almonds, pure vanilla bean, and a hint of dark rum for complexity. Double-baked to ensure the crust remains remarkably crisp while the interior stays moist and tender.
